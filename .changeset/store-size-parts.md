`kinfold size` and the dashboard Data page break the store down by part: prompts, replies, events, observations, summaries, vectors and other. Past the size warning, a line names the largest part and the setting that trims it, such as `warning: replies are 61% of 2.1 GB; set prompts retention to trim them`. Release notes now carry the version's CHANGELOG.md section, and releases come from changesets and wait for the maintainer's approval.
